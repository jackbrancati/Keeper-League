Draft Order

How the pick draft works

  1. Your finish sets your turn. Not your draft slot — just when you get to choose. The consolation winner (7th) chooses first, then 8th, 9th, 10th, then 6th all the way down to the champion, who chooses last.
  2. On your turn, you claim any open slot. Pick 1.01 through 1.10 — whatever is still on the board. You have 60 minutes to answer in the group chat or you get skipped and go to the back.
  3. That slot is your first-round pick. The rest of the draft snakes off it as normal.

Picking first in this process is not the same as having the 1.01 pick — you just get first choice of the board.
